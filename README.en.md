## Profile

I am an Angolan **senior software developer**, trained in **Computer Science**, with experience building and maintaining **web applications, native Android apps, back-end services, APIs, dashboards and relational database systems**.

I am the **founder and Executive Director of Tenda Digital Lda.** (since 2022), and have played a direct role in the architecture, development, deployment and continuous improvement of **Poligest**, a software ecosystem for educational institutions.

My main areas of work include **Java, Play Framework, JavaScript, Vue.js, PHP, SQL, MySQL/MariaDB, native Android and Flutter**, along with requirements analysis, product development, testing, deployment and production support.

I hold a **degree in Informatics, specializing in Computer Science (2018)**, and am currently pursuing a **master's degree in Human Resources Management**. I have also worked as a **university lecturer** in programming, web development and computer networking.
